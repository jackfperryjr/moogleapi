The Gauntlet

Pick a monster and fight your way up through the series, one game at a time. Beat all three battles — two enemies and a boss — and your monster evolves into that game's version of itself. Lose one and you can spend a retry; you get three for the whole run. Battles never cross games, and everyone gets the same ladder each day.
