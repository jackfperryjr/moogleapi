Sphere Hunter

Seal three fiends in spheres and take them through eleven hunts, one Final Fantasy each. Switch freely — it costs nothing and happens before your opponent moves. Take enough punishment and a sphere's Limit unlocks, once per battle — the quarry get none. Clear a hunt and you may seal the mark that guarded it, if you will give up a slot for it. Lose and you can go again straight away — every run deals a new hand and new quarry.
