Games

Four browser games built on this API. Every one of them uses public endpoints you can call yourself — no private data, no server-side state. Pick a cabinet.

Kupodle
Guess the daily Final Fantasy character in six tries. Every guess narrows it by game, role, race, hometown and release year.
Daily · Puzzle Play →
Triple Triad
The Final Fantasy VIII card game, with all 110 authentic cards and their real corner values. Three-by-three, capture by the numbers.
Cards · Versus Play →
Kupo Climb
Take one monster up through the series, a game per rung. Beat all three battles and it evolves into that game's version of itself.
Daily · Battle Play →
Battle Square
The Gold Saucer's arena, opened to the whole series. One character against eight straight waves — and the slots take something away between each.
Daily · Arena Play →

Not affiliated with Square Enix. Character and monster data comes from the Final Fantasy Wiki, served through this API.
